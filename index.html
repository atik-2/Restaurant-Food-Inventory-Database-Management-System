1. Introduction

A Restaurant & Food Inventory Management System is a database-driven application that stores and manages everything a restaurant needs in one place:

  • Food ingredients
  • Inventory stock
  • Suppliers
  • Purchase records
  • Menu items
  • Customer orders
  • Sales records
  • Employees

Instead of keeping records on paper, everything is stored in a centralized database, so restaurant operations become faster, more accurate and more efficient.

2. Problems with Traditional (Manual) Management

  • Manual record keeping
  • Lost inventory records
  • Food wastage
  • Stock shortages
  • Duplicate data
  • Slow report generation
  • Human errors
  • Difficulty tracking suppliers
Without a proper database, restaurants cannot track inventory and sales properly — this causes extra cost and poor customer service.

5. How the System Works

StepWhat happensDatabase action
1Customer places an orderINSERT INTO orders
2Order items are storedINSERT INTO order_items
3Ingredients deducted automaticallyTRIGGER → UPDATE ingredients
4Inventory is updatedstock quantity goes down
5Manager views reportsSELECT with JOIN / GROUP BY
Every customer order automatically updates the inventory. The manager always knows the current stock level without checking manually.
